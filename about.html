OUR MISSION

Why we built SkyCast
"Weather affects every decision — from what to wear to where to build. We believe that data should never be the barrier."

SkyCast was founded in 2021 by a team of meteorologists and software engineers who believed that accurate weather data should be accessible to everyone — not just researchers and enterprise customers.

We aggregate feeds from global meteorological agencies, private sensor networks, and satellite systems, then surface it in an interface that anyone can use without a science degree.
